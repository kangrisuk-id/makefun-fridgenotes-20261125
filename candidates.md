# Candidates — Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge

The brief: one funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its
owner. A single line, dry, petty and specific — the sort of thing a smug
appliance would put on its door screen. Gate: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters.

## The judge's criteria (in the order the judge weighs them)

1. **On brief** — it is a funny passive-aggressive note *from the fridge*;
   it loses to any note that is not.
2. **The joke** — a real joke in a single line, with a turn or a sting the
   reader does not see coming.
3. **The fridge's voice** — smug about its sensors, inventory and door
   alarms; petty about the owner's habits.
4. **A small grudge** — a household slight, not a rant or a threat.
5. **Fresh, concrete detail** — the yogurt, the ice maker, the leftovers —
   not generic nagging.

The judge also warns: a note that explains the joke is weaker than one that
trusts the reader, and length for its own sake does not count.

Each candidate below is scored 1–5 on each criterion (C1–C5), 25 maximum.

## Knockout

### Quarter-finals

- **#39 def. #21** — Both count stock, but #39's timestamped sensor log is
  the fridge's own voice (C3) and "I keep receipts" is a sting #21's
  warehouse line explains a beat too much.
- **#5 def. #19** — The kale memorial has the bigger single laugh, but #5
  names the door alarm and the midnight comparison (C3), and its question
  is pure fridge grievance (C1). First differing criterion decides.
- **#14 def. #2** — The support group is charming, but #14's camera and
  temperature log are smugger fridge hardware (C3), and the 2 a.m. cheese
  is the more specific crime (C5).
- **#25 def. #29** — #29's beep is a perfect door-alarm joke, but #25
  counts the crime scene — 9 condiments, 3 foods, logged at 4 degrees —
  and wins C5 by a wide margin.

### Semi-finals

- **#39 def. #5** — #5 asks its question out loud; #39 never asks anything.
  The deadpan log trusts the reader (the brief's own warning), and "third
  night" quietly escalates the grudge without enlarging it (C4).
- **#14 def. #25** — Both are inventory smugness. #14 adds a camera and a
  direct sting — "Choose your next snack wisely" — where #25's log line
  reports rather than accuses (C2).

### Final

- **#39 def. #14** — Criterion 1 is level: both are unmistakably notes from
  the fridge. On criterion 2, #14 warns the owner, and the warning leans a
  shade toward menace; #39's sting is the format itself — a security log
  that ends, flatly, with "I keep receipts." The joke is never explained,
  the grudge stays the size of one string cheese (C4), and the detail —
  2:47 a.m., string cheese, third night — is the freshest in the field
  (C5). **Winner: #39, 24/25.**

## The winning note

> Door opened: 2:47 a.m. Item taken: string cheese. This is the third night. I keep receipts.

91 characters — inside the 1–200 gate, a single line, and it reads exactly
like something a smug fridge would leave running on its door screen.
